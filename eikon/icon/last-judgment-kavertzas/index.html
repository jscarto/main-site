The Last Judgment (Kavertzas)

Tempera on wood, 124.5 × 138 cm

Depicts
Last Judgment, Second Coming, Jesus Christ
Commemorated
Sunday of the Last Judgment (Meatfare) (movable)
Period
1640–1641Frantzeskos Kavertzas
School
Cretan school
Made in
Venetian Crete (Candia)
Kept at
Hellenic Institute of Byzantine and Post-Byzantine StudiesVenice

Christ comes as judge above the cross, with angels holding the books and blowing trumpets. Below, the Archangel Michael weighs souls, the righteous enter Paradise and the river of fire carries the damned away.

Frantzeskos Kavertzas signed the panel in 1640–1641 for a nun called Eugenia. It is in the Hellenic Institute in Venice.
