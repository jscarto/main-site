St Paraskeva Pyatnitsa

Holy Great Martyr Paraskeva, called 'Friday'

Paraskeva's name means Friday, the day of the Passion, and in Russia she was called Pyatnitsa, which means the same. She was a martyr of Iconium. Women prayed to her as the protector of marriage, spinning and trade, and market days were often held under her patronage.

She is shown in a red maphorion with a cross, the sign of her martyrdom, her face severe and youthful.
