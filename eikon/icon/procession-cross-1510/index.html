The Procession of the Precious Wood of the Cross

Tempera on wood, 108 × 87 cm

Depicts
Exaltation of the Cross
Commemorated
August 1
Period
1510–1520s
School
Russian
Made in
Vladimir
Kept at
Vladimir-Suzdal Museum-ReserveVladimir

In August, the season of sickness, the relic of the Cross was carried through the streets of Constantinople. In Russia the day became a blessing of water, and the icon shows the Cross set up beside a spring with crowds coming to bathe.

It is in the Vladimir-Suzdal Museum-Reserve.
