The Mystical Supper (Kirillov, 1497)

Tempera on wood

Depicts
Holy Week, Jesus Christ, Holy Apostles
Commemorated
Great and Holy Thursday: the Mystical Supper (movable)
Period
1497
School
Kirillov (Rostov or Novgorod masters)
Made in
Kirillo-Belozersky Monastery
Kept at
State Russian MuseumSt Petersburg

Christ and the twelve recline at table, and Judas reaches into the dish. It comes from the feast tier of the Dormition Cathedral of the Kirillo-Belozersky Monastery, painted in 1497.

The icon is in the Russian Museum.
