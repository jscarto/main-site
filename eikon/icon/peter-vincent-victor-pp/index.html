Saints Peter, Vincent and Victor

Tempera on wood, painted on both sides

Depicts
St Peter, St Vincent, St Victor
Commemorated
Martyrs Victor and Vincent, November 11
Period
16th century
School
Greek
Made in
Greece
Kept at
Petit Palais, Musée des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de ParisParis

Victor and Vincent share 11 November with the great martyr Menas: Victor, a soldier martyred in Syria, and Vincent, a deacon of Spain. The Apostle Peter stands with them.

The panel is painted on both sides and is in the Petit Palais in Paris.
