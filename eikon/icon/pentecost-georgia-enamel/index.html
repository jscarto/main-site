The Descent of the Holy Spirit

Cloisonné enamel

Depicts
Pentecost, Holy Apostles
Commemorated
Holy Pentecost: the Descent of the Holy Spirit (movable)
Period
12th century
School
Georgian
Made in
Georgia
Kept at
Georgian National MuseumTbilisi

Georgian goldsmiths made some of the finest cloisonné enamels of the Middle Ages. Here the apostles sit in a semicircle as the tongues of fire descend.

It is one of the treasures of the Georgian National Museum in Tbilisi.
