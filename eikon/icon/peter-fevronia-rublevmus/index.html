Sts Peter and Fevronia of Murom

Tempera on wood

Depicts
St Peter of Murom, St Fevronia of Murom
Commemorated
Sts Peter and Fevronia, Wonderworkers of Murom, June 25
Period
16th century
School
Russian
Made in
Russia
Kept at
Andrei Rublev Museum of Early Russian ArtMoscow

Prince Peter of Murom was healed by Fevronia, a wise peasant girl, and married her against the will of his nobles. In old age both took monastic vows. They died on the same day in 1228 and were buried in one tomb.

Russians hold them up as patrons of marriage and keep their feast on 25 June.
