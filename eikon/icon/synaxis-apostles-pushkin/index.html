The Synaxis of the Twelve Apostles (Constantinople)

Tempera on wood

Depicts

School

Made in

The twelve apostles stand together in a crowded, animated group, each distinguished by his face. This is one of the finest Palaiologan icons in Russia, though damaged.

It is in the Pushkin Museum in Moscow.
