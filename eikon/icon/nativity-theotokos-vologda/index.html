The Nativity of the Theotokos, with Scenes from the Life of Joachim and Anna

Tempera on wood, 169.5 × 124 cm

Depicts
Nativity of the Theotokos, St Joachim, St Anna, Theotokos
Commemorated
September 8
Period
1565–1566
School
Vologda
Made in
Vologda
Kept at
Vologda Museum-ReserveVologda

The birth of the Mother of God fills the center, and the border tells the story of her parents Joachim and Anna and her own childhood, with feasts of the year.

The great panel, painted by Sukhoi Milovo, came from the church of St Leontius in Vologda and is in the Vologda museum.
