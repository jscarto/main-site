Theotokos

The Hodegetria (mosaic icon, Sinai)

Mosaic, 44.6 × 33 cm

Depicts
Theotokos, Theotokos Hodegetria
Period
Early 13th century
School
Byzantine
Made in
Constantinople
Kept at
St Catherine's Monastery, Mount SinaiSinai, Egypt

The Mother of God holds the Child and points to him: 'she who shows the way'. This icon is made of tiny tesserae of stone, glass and gold set in wax, so that it glitters in candlelight.

Portable mosaic icons were among the most precious Byzantine objects. This one is at Sinai.
