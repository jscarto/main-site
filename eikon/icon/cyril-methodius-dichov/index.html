The brothers Cyril and Methodius of Thessaloniki carried the Gospel to the Slavs in the ninth century. They devised an alphabet for the Slavic tongue and translated the Scriptures and services into it.

The Church calls them Equal-to-the-Apostles and the Teachers of the Slavs. This icon by Avram Dichov belongs to the Church of Sts Peter and Paul in Tresonče, a village of the Debar painters.
