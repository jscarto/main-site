Daniel was a disciple of Symeon the Stylite. He lived on a pillar near Constantinople for 33 years, where emperors and patriarchs climbed up to ask his counsel. He died in 493.

The tall, narrow panel suits its subject. It comes from the Cathedral of the Nativity of the Mother of God at Ferapontov Monastery, painted by the circle of Dionisius.
