Eusebius, bishop of Samosata, defended the faith of Nicaea, helped make Basil the Great a bishop, and was killed in 380 by an Arian woman who threw a roof tile at his head.

This is a detail of a menaion icon of the first quarter of the seventeenth century, from central Russia, which shows all the saints of one month in rows. The set is kept by the Church Archaeological Cabinet of the Moscow Theological Academy.
