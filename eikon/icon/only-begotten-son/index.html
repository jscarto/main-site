Only-begotten Son (late 17th century)

This icon illustrates the hymn 'Only-begotten Son and Word of God', sung at every Liturgy. Christ is crucified beneath the heavens rolled up like a scroll, in the ornate late Muscovite manner.

The icon is in the Tretyakov Gallery.
