The Miracle of the Archangel Michael at Chonae

Tempera on wood, 37.5 × 30.7 cm

Depicts
Archangel Michael, St Archippus
Commemorated
September 6
Period
First half of the 12th century
School
Constantinople (probably)
Made in
Constantinople
Kept at
St Catherine's Monastery, Mount SinaiSinai, Egypt

Pagans tried to destroy the church of the hermit Archippus at Chonae by diverting two rivers against it. The Archangel Michael struck the rock with his staff and sent the waters into a cleft, and the church was saved.
