Deesis with the Martyr Phanourios

Tempera on wood, 24.4 × 16.9 cm

Depicts
St Phanourios, Deesis
Commemorated
Holy Great Martyr Phanourios, August 27
Period
15th century
School
Greek
Made in

Kept at
St Catherine's Monastery, Mount SinaiSinai, Egypt

Phanourios is known only from an icon found in the ruins of a church on Rhodes in the fourteenth century. Greeks pray to him to 'reveal' lost things, and bake a phanouropita in thanksgiving.

Here he stands below a Deesis on a small icon at Sinai.
