Four Panels from a Pair of Doors

John the Theologian and Prochoros, the Baptism, the Descent into Hades, St Nicholas

Tempera on wood

Depicts
St John the Theologian, Theophany, Resurrection, St Nicholas
Commemorated

Period
Early 15th century
School
Russian (Novgorod?)

Kept at
The Metropolitan Museum of ArtNew York

These four scenes were once painted on the leaves of a pair of doors, perhaps from a small iconostasis or a cabinet for relics. At top left St John dictates his Gospel to his disciple Prochoros on the island of Patmos, his head turned to listen to the voice of God.
