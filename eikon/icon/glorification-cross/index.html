The Glorification of the Cross

Reverse of the Novgorod Mandylion

Tempera on wood, reverse of the Mandylion

Depicts
Exaltation of the Cross, Holy Angels
Commemorated
September 14
Third Sunday of Lent: Veneration of the Holy Cross (movable)
August 1
Period
Second half of the 12th century
School
Novgorod
Made in
Novgorod
Kept at
State Tretyakov GalleryMoscow

On the back of the Holy Face, angels venerate the cross on Golgotha, holding the spear and the reed with the sponge, the instruments of the Passion. Above, cherubim and seraphim fill the sky.

The icon was probably carried in procession, so the faithful saw the face of Christ and the cross of his sacrifice in turn. The cross stands above a small dark cave holding Adam's skull. Tradition places Adam's burial at Golgotha, beneath the cross that saves him.
