The Cross of Kiy (Saltanov, 1670)

Tempera on wood

Depicts
Exaltation of the Cross, St Constantine, St Helen
Commemorated
September 14
Period
1670Ivan Saltanov
School
Moscow (Kremlin Armoury)
Made in

Kept at
State Historical MuseumMoscow

The Cross of Kiy was a replica of the True Cross filled with relics, made for Patriarch Nikon in 1656. Constantine, Tsar Alexei Mikhailovich and Nikon stand on one side, and Helena and Tsarina Maria on the other.

Ivan Saltanov, a court painter, made this icon of it. It is in the State Historical Museum, Moscow.
