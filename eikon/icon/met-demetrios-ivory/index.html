Ivory Icon of St Demetrios

The soldier-martyr of Thessaloniki

Demetrios stands in the armor of a Roman officer, his name carved beside his head, holding a sword and resting his hand on a tall shield. In the earliest images he was shown as a young aristocrat in a cloak. From around this time, Byzantine art increasingly showed him as a warrior saint, the defender of Thessaloniki.

The icon is kept at the Met Cloisters in northern Manhattan.
