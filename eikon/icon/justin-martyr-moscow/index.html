Justin searched through the schools of philosophy before he found Christ, and then kept the philosopher's cloak to teach the faith. His defenses of Christianity are the earliest that survive at length. He was beheaded in Rome around 165.

The icon is in the Church of the Resurrection on Uspensky Vrazhek in Moscow.
