The Entry into Jerusalem

Festal tier, Cathedral of the Annunciation, Moscow Kremlin

Tempera on wood, 81 × 63 cm

Depicts
Palm Sunday, Jesus Christ
Commemorated
Entry of Our Lord into Jerusalem (Palm Sunday) (movable)
Period
Early 15th centuryAttributed to Andrei Rublev
School
Moscow
Made in
Moscow
Kept at
Cathedral of the Annunciation, Moscow KremlinMoscow

Christ rides into the city on a colt, turning back to his disciples, while the people come out with branches and children spread garments on the road.

The feast opens Holy Week. In Russia, where palms do not grow, the faithful hold branches of pussy willow, which are blessed at the vigil the evening before.
